# S3 PLOWLANE

Take the plow and stay in the lane for the whole leg. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3plowlane/

## Design

Stay in the lane for the whole leg, ahead of the other crew. One departure fails it.
