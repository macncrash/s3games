# S3 HEADERLANE

Take the header and stay in the lane for the whole leg. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3headerlane/

## Design

Take the header, hold the lane, and finish under the other crew. One departure fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
