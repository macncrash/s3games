# S3 STANDARD

Take the flag off the road and bring it back.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3standard/

## Design

A regimental standard is standing in the roadway. Reaching it is not the job. The colour has to come off the road and back through your own lines. A drop plants it in the road again.

Arrows steer. C or Up is gas. X, Q, or Down brakes. Space turns the bike about once it is slow. Start rides.
