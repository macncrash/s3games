# S3 WHARFPACE

At the wharf, you wait until the third pace before you fire. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3wharfpace/

## Design

Fire on the third pace. An early shot is not done.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
