# S3 CULVERTRELI

At the culvert, you hold until the relief bell. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3culvertreli/

## Design

Hold until the relief bell. Leaving early is a loss.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
