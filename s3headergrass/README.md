# S3 HEADERGRASS

Take the header and land on the grass and come to a full stop. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3headergrass/

## Design

Land on the grass and come to a full stop ahead of the other crew. Sliding off the grass fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
