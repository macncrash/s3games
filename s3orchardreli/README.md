# S3 ORCHARDRELI

At the orchard, you hold until the relief bell. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3orchardreli/

## Design

Hold the orchard until the relief bell. Surviving the watch and missing the bell is not relief.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
