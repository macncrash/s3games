# S3 RICKSHAWBOOM

Take the rickshaw and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3rickshawboom/

## Design

Deliver the drive to the boom ahead of the other crew. Short of the boom fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
