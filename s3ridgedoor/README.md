# S3 RIDGEDOOR

At the ridge, you hold the door for three minutes. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3ridgedoor/

## Design

The door has to hold for three minutes. Opening it early ends the watch.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
