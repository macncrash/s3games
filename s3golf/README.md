# S3 GOLF

Three holes. In the hole, not on the green.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3golf/

## Design

In the hole, not on the green. Three holes, and a ball next to the cup is the same as a ball in the trees.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
