# S3 BUNKERRELI

You have the bunker. The job is to hold until the relief bell. Anything else is a loss.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3bunkerreli/

## Design

Hold the bunker until the relief bell. Surviving the watch and missing the bell is not relief.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
