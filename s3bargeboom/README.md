# S3 BARGEBOOM

Take the barge and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3bargeboom/

## Design

The drive has to land on the boom ahead of the other crew. A late delivery fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
