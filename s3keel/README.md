# S3 KEEL

Sail a triangle of buoys and come back to the same dock.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3keel/

## Design

The triangle brings you back to the dock you left. A buoy you miss is not a penalty lap you can ignore. You have to come around and take it.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
