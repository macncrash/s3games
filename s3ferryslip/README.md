# S3 FERRYSLIP

Take the ferry and berth in the slip before the tide turns. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3ferryslip/

## Design

Berth in the slip before the tide turns, ahead of the other crew. A late berth fails the leg.
