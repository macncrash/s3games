# S3 REDOUBTBANN

At the redoubt, you bring the banner back. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3redoubtbann/

## Design

Bring the banner back to the redoubt. Stopping short of home is not done.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
