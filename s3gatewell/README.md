# S3 GATEWELL

You have the gate. The job is to keep the well standing through three waves. Anything else is a loss.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3gatewell/

## Design

The well has to stand through three waves. A stone that gets through is a loss even if you are still at the gate.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
