# S3 BIKEBOOM

Take the bike and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3bikeboom/

## Design

Deliver the drive to the boom before the other crew. Short of the boom fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
