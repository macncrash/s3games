# S3 GATERELI

At the gate, you hold until the relief bell. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3gatereli/

## Design

Hold the gate until the relief bell, then answer it. Surviving the watch and missing the bell is not relief.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
