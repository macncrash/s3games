# S3 LUGEBOOM

Take the luge and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3lugeboom/

## Design

The drive has to sit on the boom ahead of the other crew. Reaching the boom without setting it down fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
