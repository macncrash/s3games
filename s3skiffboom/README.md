# S3 SKIFFBOOM

Take the skiff and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3skiffboom/

## Design

The drive has to land on the boom ahead of the other crew. A late delivery fails the leg even if it arrives.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
