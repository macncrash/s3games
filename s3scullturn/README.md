# S3 SCULL TURN

In the scull, you make the three turns without tipping. Missing the end fails the leg.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3scullturn/

## Play

Arrows steer. Up raises the rating, down checks it. Hold Z to set the boat through a bend. Reach the end of the leg still upright, with every turn made. A tip, a skipped bend, or a missed finish fails the leg.
