# S3 KEELTURN

Take the keel and make the three turns without tipping. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3keelturn/

## Design

Make the three turns without tipping, ahead of the other crew. One tip fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
