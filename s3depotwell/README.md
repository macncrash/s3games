# S3 DEPOTWELL

At the depot, you keep the well standing through three waves. Miss that and the watch is over.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3depotwell/

## Design

The well has to stand through three waves. A breach is a loss even if you are still at the depot.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
