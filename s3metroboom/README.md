# S3 METROBOOM

Take the metro and deliver the drive to the boom. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3metroboom/

## Design

Deliver the drive to the boom ahead of the other crew. Late is a loss.
