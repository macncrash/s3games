# S3 HELIGRASS

Take the heli and land on the grass and come to a full stop. The clock is the other crew.

Play it in the browser: https://macncrash.github.io/s3games/play/s3heligrass/

## Design

Land on the grass and come to a full stop. Rolling past the grass fails the leg.

The browser build is one file. The link above is that file, not a capture of a desktop session.
